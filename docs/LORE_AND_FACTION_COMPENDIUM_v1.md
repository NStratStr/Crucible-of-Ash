# CRUCIBLE OF ASH: WORLD LOGIC & FACTION COMPENDIUM
## Master Design Document — Volume I: Lore, Metaphysics, and Factions

---

## 1. Cosmological Foundation & The Great Rupture

### 1.1 The Primordial Resource: Vitae
Long before the ash smothered the horizon, civilization was powered by a single planetary fuel: **Vitae**.

- **Nature:** A dense, glowing, crimson fluid running through deep geological aquifers (the *Veins of the Earth*). Vitae holds concentrated memory, biological vitality, and metaphysical mass.
- **Pre-Cataclysm Society:** Empires tapped Vitae wellheads like petroleum to power industrial smelting, run street-lighting grids, cure diseases, and animate civil-works golems. It was a well-understood, heavily contested natural resource, not an obscure occult secret.

### 1.2 The Cataclysm: The Rupture & The White Rain
The disaster was a planetary failure triggered by imperial hubris:

1. **The Ignition:** Imperial artificers constructed the **Undying Hearth**—a vast subterranean facility designed to ignite an artificial internal sun for perpetual energy.
2. **The Wellhead Rupture:** The ignition drilled through the crust and ruptured the planetary mantle, superheating a massive, pressurized primordial Vitae seam. The resulting pressure wave blasted billions of tons of liquefied mineral bedrock, boiling Vitae, and pulverized soul-matter miles into the upper atmosphere.
3. **The White Rain:** As the vaporized plume cooled in the stratosphere, it fell back to earth as a caustic, petrifying fallout: **The White Rain**.

```
[ THE DEEP DRILL ] ──► Pierces the Mantle & Superheats Vitae Seam
                              │
                              ▼
[ PLANETARY ERUPTION ] ──► Vaporizes Bedrock, Minerals & Soul-Marrow into Stratosphere
                              │
                              ▼
[ THE WHITE RAIN ]     ──► Churning, petrifying fallout coats the planet in caustic ash
```

### 1.3 The Two Fates: The Calcified vs. The Ash-Veined
- **The Calcified Majority (99%):** Suffered instant pulmonary calcification. Inhaling the fallout turned flesh, blood, and lung tissue to brittle chalk and porous mortar. Millions froze in place as fragile, hollow husks that now erode across the wastes. Their souls remain trapped inside or drift through the air as restless, screaming soul-embers.
- **The Ash-Veined Adaptation (The Survivors):** A minuscule fraction of survivors—principally well-miners, refinery alchemists, and residents near raw volcanic fissures—carried a high natural tolerance to raw Vitae. When the ash fell, their bodies absorbed the mineral particulate into their marrow and vascular walls rather than rejecting it.
- **The Living Paradox:** Ash-Veined survivors have thickened, quicksilver-heavy crimson blood (Natural Vitae) and hardened, stone-tough knuckles. They are completely immune to airborne ash toxicity, but channeling their will to sculpt constructs slowly hardens their own capillaries. They must continually secure fresh Vitae to sustain their bodies.

## 2. Metaphysics of Form & Combat

### 2.1 The Material: "Flesh of Stone" (Mineral Sinew)
Constructs in Crucible of Ash are neither fragile drywall statues nor steam-powered robots with gears and pistons.

- **Organic Mineral Physics:** When porous mineral matter (ash, bone, iron, or chalk) is saturated with pressurized Vitae, it undergoes an alchemical phase change. It behaves like petrified muscle—possessing the density and weight of rock, but the elasticity, speed, and responsiveness of living muscle fibers.
- **Bleed Mechanics:** When struck, a construct’s outer glaze cracks, causing pressurized, glowing internal Vitae to physically spray and pool on the ground.
- **Lifesteal & Feeding:** Porous, ravenous weapons drink leaking Vitae through capillary action on contact, lubricating and re-hydrating the attacking construct's seizing stone sinews.
- **Desiccation (The "Ghoul" State):** Constructs starved of Vitae dry out into hunched, brittle shells whose joints grind audibly, driving them into a frenzied hunt to crack open other vessels and scrape out congealed fluid.

### 2.2 The Plinth: The Kinetic Grounding Seal
- **Functional Field, Not a Rock:** A unit’s circular base is an active, downward-radiating field of pressurized Vitae and magical tension that burns a crisp boundary ring into the earth.
- **Physical Necessity:** Without this localized gravitational anchor, high-density constructs dissolve back into drifting ash. The ring grounds their three-dimensional mass into physical reality while allowing full biometric freedom of movement.
- **Visual Anchor:** The plinth visually communicates faction alignment, team ownership, collision radius, and tactical footing.

## 3. The Commander’s Apparatus & The Battlefield

### 3.1 The Catalyst Key & The Gauntlet
- **The Apparatus:** Commanders wear an Alchemical Gauntlet fitted with a Catalyst Key—an imperial master-component made of brass gear-trains, volcanic glass prisms, and an internal perpetual Vitae core.
- **Instant Flash-Condensation:** The commander channels their Focus and feeds a measure of Vitae through the Key into the ground. Ambient ash and mineral matter violently snap into the radiant plinth vortex, manifesting a full-scale, combat-ready construct within three seconds.

### 3.2 Tactical Bullet-Time & Tuning Slips
- **The "Cards":** Tactical rites are stored on Tuning Slips—thin plates of slate-foil, pressed horn, or rolled gold leaf etched with micro-runic circuits.
- **Execution:** Sliding a slip into the gauntlet’s chamber fires a burst of pressurized Vitae across the circuit, flashing the command into the constructs' grounding seals. Spent slips drop into the under-rail Burn Pit to cycle and cool.
- **Bullet-Time:** Manipulating cards slows local time by 30% to represent the commander projecting their neural tether across the battlefield miasma.

## 4. Universal Progression, Economy & The Crucible

### 4.1 Summoning: Unsealing Relics
- **Universal G1 Baseline:** Every construct enters the game at Generation 1 (G1) with zero baseline stat rarity tiers. Every unit possesses a viable evolutionary path to a G6 Sovereign.
- **The Discovery Loop:** Commanders salvage Calcified Relics and Sealed Memory Slips (petrified helmets, beast horns, rolled slate-tablets) from ruins and boss nodes.
- **Awakening:** Inserting an unidentified relic into the Catalyst Key and pulsing it with Vitae burns away the calcified crust, reads the dormant combat frequency, and condenses the unit on the spot.

### 4.2 The Economy: Clean & Non-Fragmented
- **Unbound Ash (Soft Currency / Mass):** Harvested from scrap and combat; spent to level up base stats and enhance physical gear (+1 to +10).
- **Distilled Vitae (Reagent / Fuel):** Harvested from subterranean springs; spent to power Crucible fusions, unlock ascensions, and cast high-tier rites.

### 4.3 The Crucible: 100% Deterministic Evolution
- **Binary Fusions:** Merging requires identical chassis copies ($G1 + G1 \to G2$, up to $32 \times G1 = 1 \times G6$).
- **Guaranteed Power:** Fusions never roll random baseline stats or overwrite socketed traits. Reaching G6 guarantees maximum base stat ceilings and unlocks all empty plinth sockets.
- **Visual Scale:** Units operate with a Standard Form (G1–G4) and an expanded Apex Sovereign Form (G5–G6) featuring polished materials, radiant particle embers, and deeper plinth glows.

### 4.4 The Endgame Chase: Traits, Gear & Boss Reagents
- **Sockets & Soul-Chisels:** Units unlock 1 to 3 trait sockets in their plinth rings as their generation increases. Traits are extracted from donor units or forged from boss materials, then upgraded deterministically using Ash and Vitae.
- **Target-Farmed Boss Reagents ("Anima Slag"):** Major bosses drop dedicated crafting materials (e.g., *Molten Brim-Slag*, *Virulent Bile-Cores*, *Phase-Fractured Glass*) used to craft specific high-tier keystone traits.

## 5. The Playable Faction Structure

The game’s four primary playable factions span a clear spectrum from Unit-Heavy Martial Combat to Focus-Heavy Pure Magic:

```
[ 20% MAGIC / 80% UNIT ] ─────────────────────────────────────────► [ 80% MAGIC / 20% UNIT ]
     Ash Legion            Crypt Stalkers          Mire Coven               Calx Order
  (Cold Iron & Stone)    (Marble & Blood-Silk)   (Bog-Iron & Fungi)     (Pure Chalk & Storms)
```

### FACTION 1: THE ASH LEGION
> *"Order Carved in Iron, Sustained by the Forge."*

- **Ideology & Culture:** The surviving remnants of the imperial military high command and sun-smith guilds. They view the Cataclysm not as the end of civilization, but as a total collapse of imperial discipline. Their doctrine is martial, dogmatic, and stoic: fortify the highways, purge feral anomalies, and conquer ancient Crucible Altars to reignite the true sun.
- **Aesthetic & Materials:** Chiseled granite ashlar blocks, pitted imperial iron, heavy rivets, scorched white and saffron tabards, smoldering coals glowing behind breastplate grates.
- **Plinth Seal:** A heavy, square-stamped iron rim with glowing orange forge-marks and runic defensive chevrons.
- **Combat Mechanics:**
  - Heavy Physical Damage Reduction (PRR) diminishing-returns armor.
  - Frontline shield bashes that inflict Poise Stagger.
  - Retaliatory thorns damage and radiant solar burns.
  - Low Focus consumption; tactical cards provide defensive stances and formation buffs.
- **Signature Commander: Lady Valeria (The Sun-Scorched Justiciar)**
  - *Deck Limit:* Compact 10-Slip Magazine.
  - *Silhouette:* Towering granite-and-plate full armor, carrying a two-handed executioner's greatsword that leaves molten trails in the air.
  - *Playstyle:* Frontline anchor who redirects incoming damage to her own armor and converts ally Stagger into squad-wide radiant shield barriers.

---

### FACTION 2: THE CRYPT STALKERS
> *"Lineage is Eternal; Flesh is Merely the Goblet."*

- **Ideology & Culture:** The imperial patricians and noble bloodlines who sealed themselves in the deep subterranean mausoleums beneath the capital. Addicted to Vitae long before the fall, they view the surface wastes as an uncivilized ruin and other survivors as cattle. They conduct endless, decadent court ceremonies behind lead-sealed vault doors, driven by an urgent need for fresh fluid to prevent their bodies from seizing.
- **Aesthetic & Materials:** Polished black obsidian marble, carved ivory bone-plates, silver filigree, melting crimson wax seals, frayed moth-eaten mourning velvet, surgical needle-blades.
- **Plinth Seal:** A dark, mirror-polished obsidian circular well with weeping arterial channels where surplus crimson Vitae pools and recirculates.
- **Combat Mechanics:**
  - High critical strike chance and execution burst windows.
  - Rapid Bleed infliction that scales damage over time.
  - Direct Vitae lifesteal that siphons enemy health to repair stone sinews.
  - Balanced tactical cards focused on health sacrifices, target assassination, and emergency revives.
- **Signature Commander: Lord Vladislav (The Sanguine Sovereign)**
  - *Deck Limit:* Standard 12-Slip Magazine.
  - *Silhouette:* Slender, aristocratic figure draped in high-collared velvet shrouds, wielding a silver rapier fitted with a hollow Vitae conduit.
  - *Playstyle:* Sacrifices his own constructs' current HP to trigger high-damage burst executions, recovering his investment as bonus Vitae whenever a bleeding target dies.

---

### FACTION 3: THE MIRE COVEN
> *"From Putrefaction, the Seed Unfurls."*

- **Ideology & Culture:** The pariahs of the empire—lowland peat-cutters, herbal alchemists, and outcasts who inhabited the deep swamps where the ash fell thin. They hold a unique metaphysical belief: calcification is an unnatural paralysis. To them, stone must rot, decompose, and return to fertile soil for life to truly start anew. They live in shadowy communal covens, brewing caustic tinctures in copper cauldrons and worshiping the beauty of fungal decay.
- **Aesthetic & Materials:** Dense peat clay, rusted and pitted bog-iron, weeping petrified roots, tarnished copper censers, sickly yellow-green bioluminescent fungal shelves, hanging vials of murky bile.
- **Plinth Seal:** A churned ring of wet bog-mud sprouting shelf fungi, oozing viridian bubbles, and calcified root tendrils.
- **Combat Mechanics:**
  - Stacking Poison DoTs that bypass physical armor.
  - Attrition debuffs that decay enemy Attack, Armor, and Speed stats over time.
  - Caustic puddle hazards that damage units entering affected rows.
  - High-magic reliance; tactical cards detonate poison stacks and trigger corpse explosions.
- **Signature Commander: Malakor (Father of Rot)**
  - *Deck Limit:* 14-Slip Magazine.
  - *Silhouette:* Hunched, root-bound figure draped in stained burlap, bearing a cluster of smoking, perforated copper censers bound across his back.
  - *Playstyle:* Area-of-effect controller who spreads contagion across entire enemy rows and consumes poison stacks for catastrophic burst damage.

---

### FACTION 4: THE CALX ORDER
> *"We Do Not Walk Upon the Earth; We Command the Sky."*

- **Ideology & Culture:** The high atmospheric chymists and astronomers who occupied the mountain observatory spires during the Cataclysm. Rather than hiding underground, they embraced the airborne fallout. They realized the White Rain is a suspended, hyper-reactive aerosol of pure mineral potential. They view terrestrial dirt, iron, and mud as crude impurities, weaving their creations directly out of the calcified sky.
- **Aesthetic & Materials:** Compressed white chalk (calx), blinding powdered lime, woven bleached linen, translucent alabaster plates, spinning platinum barometer-dials, suspended dust motes.
- **Plinth Seal:** A stark, chalk-white compass ring etched into the earth, generating swirling miniature dust-devils that float upward against gravity.
- **Combat Mechanics:**
  - Pure Focus/Magic dominance; low base unit armor balanced by high spell reliance.
  - Flash-Petrification mechanics that freeze enemy joints and delay their turn on the 30-tick clock.
  - Swirling chalk barriers (Aegis shields) that absorb kinetic and spell impacts.
  - Rapid tactical card cycling with heavy turn manipulation and armor erosion.
- **Signature Commander: Morrigan, the Pale Mother (The Sky-Chymist)**
  - *Deck Limit:* **Expansive 20-Slip Magazine** with a 4-card active hand and hyper-rapid Focus recovery.
  - *Silhouette:* Elegant, faceless silhouette veiled behind falling white ash powder, wrapped in layered alabaster robes and wearing a platinum weather-bracer.
  - *Playstyle:* High-tempo spell-slinger who cycles cheap tactical slips to manipulate the initiative timeline, freeze dangerous vanguard threats, and shatter brittle targets with kinetic pulses.

## 6. Non-Playable Factions & World Hazards

### 6.1 The Main Antagonist: The Pyre Synod
- **Role:** The primary campaign enemy faction and world bosses.
- **Lore:** Fanatical ascetics and flagellants who believe the Cataclysm was interrupted prematurely. They view the half-calcified state of the world as a stagnant limbo and seek to ignite the remaining subterranean Vitae reserves, completely incinerating the planet to pure, clean charcoal.
- **Aesthetic:** Charred black timber, smoldering coal cores, iron chains, heavy soot clouds, smoldering orange embers.
- **Combat Style:** Extreme suicidal burst damage, sacrificial attacks, battlefield flame hazards, and explosive enrage phases (such as *Gorgoroth the Flayed*).

### 6.2 The Wild Host: Corrupted Beasts
- **Role:** Neutral map nodes, hunting grounds, and unaligned tameable armatures.
- **Lore:** Ancient wildlife (dire boars, basilisks, predatory hounds) that survived the fallout by secreting acidic glandular fluids that fused falling ash directly into thick, calcified slag carapaces. They hunt constructs because circulating Vitae channels smell like warm blood.
- **Gameplay Function:** Players defeat beast packs at "Hunt Nodes" to farm rare crafting reagents (Anima Slag) and record their primal armatures into their Catalyst Keys, allowing them to manifest unaligned beast tanks and skirmishers in any squad.
